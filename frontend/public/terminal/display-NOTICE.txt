The terminal display bundles these third-party components. Build them with frontend/bin/terminal-display/build.sh.

linux-fb-bzimage.bin
    Linux 5.6.15, GPL-2.0 WITH Linux-syscall-note. See linux-COPYING.txt and linux-GPL-2.0.txt.
    Source: https://cdn.kernel.org/pub/linux/kernel/v5.x/linux-5.6.15.tar.xz
    Configuration: frontend/bin/terminal-display/kernel.config
    Embeds the initramfs of the v86 Buildroot image (https://i.copy.sh/buildroot-bzimage.bin, Buildroot 2020.05).

    Source: https://musl.libc.org/releases/musl-1.2.5.tar.gz

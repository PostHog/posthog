# Pulse history in subscription delivery history

When the existing Pulse feature flag is enabled, an AI report subscription can show read-only follow-up recommendations below its delivery history.

The history shows why each recommendation matters now, its confidence, effort, expected metric direction and movement, supporting evidence, prepared work, adoption time, and one-time metric comparison. Pending comparisons show when their readout is due. Values are absolute metric totals and deltas, not a causal claim.

Pulse history is available only to people who can view both the subscription and its query data.

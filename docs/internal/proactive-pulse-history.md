# Pulse history in subscription delivery history

When the existing Pulse feature flag is enabled, an AI report subscription can show read-only follow-up recommendations below its delivery history.

The history shows the recommendation, its supporting evidence, prepared work and adoption time, and a one-time metric comparison. Pending comparisons show when their readout is due. Values are absolute metric totals and deltas, not a confidence score or a causal claim.

Pulse history is available only to people who can view both the subscription and its query data.

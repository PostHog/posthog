**Metrics & SQL trust**: any count, rate, average, percentile, cost per unit, or conversion — of anything the product records, not just KPIs — is a metric question. Inspect the complete governed catalog with `metric-list` before `read-data-schema`, insights, domain tools, or raw SQL. Use `metric-describe` for a candidate's definition, including HogQL or SQL, before adapting it. Run `learn analytics` before writing SQL or joins — it covers certified tables/views and verified joins. State the catalog outcome in query context.

Scope and limitations

This page calls the real Go autocomplete and validate endpoints. It does not run SQL or compare results with Django.

CTE and subquery examples help expose gaps in the service. The page displays its responses without filling in suggestions itself.

The catalog is a hand-maintained subset. Virtual joins, all product tables, and project-specific schemas are not modeled completely.

Enable Analyze as you type to validate and complete after a short typing pause, or use the buttons and shortcuts. Click a suggestion to insert it. Results clear when you edit the query or move the completion cursor.

Follow-up work and exclusions are tracked in docs/internal/hogql-language-service-demo.md.
